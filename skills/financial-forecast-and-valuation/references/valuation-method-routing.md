# Valuation Method Routing

Use this as a decision aid, not a mandatory formula list.

| Project condition | Primary question | Suitable methods | Do not overclaim |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pre-revenue technology | What milestone and financing path can create value? | venture method, scorecard, relevant transactions, milestone scenarios | DCF output is highly assumption-sensitive; do not present it as market value |
| Early revenue with weak repeatability | Can revenue and margin become repeatable? | revenue/GMV multiple, venture method, scenario DCF | Pipeline is not revenue; use conversion and evidence ranges |
| Growth with repeatable cohorts/orders | What is sustainable cash flow and growth? | DCF, revenue or EBITDA multiples, transaction comparables | Normalize metric definitions and avoid mixing ARR, bookings, and revenue |
| Mature or asset-heavy | What cash flow and asset base support value? | DCF, EBITDA/EBIT multiples, asset/transaction comparables, LBO returns | Keep maintenance capex, debt, working capital, and tax visible |
| Strategic manufacturing or project business | What capacity, backlog and unit economics can be delivered? | capacity/revenue build, DCF, transaction or EBITDA multiples | Market TAM does not prove company revenue; reconcile orders, capacity and delivery |

For all methods, state the valuation date, metric period, currency, entity scope, net debt/cash, fully diluted share basis, and source for the multiple or discount rate. Present a range when any of these is uncertain.
