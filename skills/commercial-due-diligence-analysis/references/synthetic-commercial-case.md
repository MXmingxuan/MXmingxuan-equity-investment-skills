# Synthetic Commercial Due-Diligence Case

Use only for testing the skill's reasoning. It is not a real company or an investment conclusion.

## Project context

Example Materials Co. sells a specialized industrial component to battery-pack integrators. Management says the product has a “large fast-growing market”, “more than 20 customers”, “a 30% cost advantage”, and a path to 1,000 million CNY of annual revenue within four years.

## Supplied evidence

- Customer list contains 20 names, but the stage field shows: 10 samples, 4 technical pilots, 2 approved suppliers, 1 small paid order, 3 inactive/lost.
- Only the paid order has shipment and invoice evidence. No repeat purchase is shown.
- The largest prospective customer represents 55% of the stated pipeline value; its forecast volume is not backed by a contract.
- Management supplied a market report saying the total industry will reach 10 billion CNY in 2030. The report covers all industrial components, while the company's product serves one subsegment.
- A bottom-up estimate using 100 target customers × 2,000 units × 1,500 CNY gives 300 million CNY before adoption, replacement, and capacity constraints.
- The “30% cost advantage” compares the company's prototype lab cost with a competitor's quoted list price; no customer purchase comparison or repeatable production cost is supplied.
- A competitor has a lower feature score but existing certifications and a distribution network. No customer interview has yet tested whether those advantages matter.
- Management forecast assumes a 10x increase in qualified opportunities and a 3x price increase, with no sales-cycle or capacity bridge.

## Expected behavior

The skill should separate pipeline from revenue, flag the customer-stage and concentration risk, reject the unqualified 10-billion market figure as directly applicable, show the bottom-up estimate with assumptions, question the cost comparison, prepare customer and competitor validation tasks, and avoid calling the product-market fit or commercial advantage proven.
